Illustrated Paint tools

Paint's 24px tool artwork is adapted from GIMP, KolourPaint, and Font Awesome Free 6.7.2. Changes by gsnv-paint, November 15, 2026: native-size rasterization, hard pixel coverage, stronger colour contrast, a gold lasso and gold wand stars, and one-pixel black contours inside the objects. Dark details are reinforced; white selection dashes are transparent. Original source geometry is retained in the downloadable artwork.

GIMP contributors include Jakub Steiner, Klaus Staedtler, Andreas Nilsson and Barak Itkin. Original per-file notices are preserved: selections, lasso, brush, blur, smudge and text use CC BY-SA 2.0; move, pencil, clone and gradient use CC BY-SA 4.0; the bucket retains its original GPL 2.0 notice. Derivatives retain these licenses. GIMP theme notice.

KolourPaint artwork is credited to Nuno Pinheiro, Danny Allen, Clarence Dang and Kristof Borrey, under the project's BSD redistribution terms and copyright notice. Font Awesome's wand is copyright 2024 Fonticons, Inc., under CC BY 4.0. Font Awesome original notice.
